/*
 * DJ presentation stacks one slot per current play, in play order, anchored at the bottom. A slot's
 * height follows its --presence, so the stack opens as a panel rises into it and closes as one
 * sinks out of it. The vertical clip keeps a moving panel off its neighbors. Chrome applies
 * overflow-clip-margin only when both axes clip, so --slot-bleed does not reach past it: a theme
 * keeps shadows and ornaments above or below a panel inside the panel's padding.
 *
 * The slot opens and closes in whole pixels from its full size, so its panel and those around it
 * only ever move by whole pixels. Chrome does not repaint the contents of a composited panel (one

 * last painted, and each render tab last painted it on a different frame of the entrance.
